Team Delta · Pulse Health

One topic in. A checked, captioned Reel out.

A chain of Claude Code skills that writes, fact-checks, voices, animates and captions 30–60 second Instagram Reels for a preventive-care brand, with a medical compliance gate in front of every publish.

The whole pipeline is one command
# topic, client and mode in; reel.mp4 + post.txt out ./generate.sh \ --topic "When a headache means see a doctor" \ --client pulse-health --mode character

The compliance gate

Hard mode for healthcare. It scans the script, every scene line, every visual prompt and the post, and the run stops until it passes.

RuleBlocksAllowed instead
no-diagnosis"You have high blood pressure.""If you have high blood pressure, talk to your doctor about a plan."
no-cure-claim"This walk cures high blood pressure.""Regular activity can lower your risk of heart disease and stroke."
no-unsafe-advice"No need to see a doctor for this.""If any of these match, talk to a doctor right away."
credible-sourceA script or post whose only source is not who.int, cdc.gov or nhs.uk. Look-alike domains are rejected.NHS and CDC pages, cited in the caption with links.
disclaimerNo "Not medical advice" card on screen or line in the post.A silent end card and the caption line, on every Reel.
caption-contrastCaption colours under WCAG 4.5:1.White and apricot on deep teal, checked in code.
