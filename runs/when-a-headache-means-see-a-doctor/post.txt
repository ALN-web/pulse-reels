Most headaches pass on their own. These five red flags are worth taking seriously.

1. A sudden, severe headache, the worst you have ever felt: call for help right away.
2. A headache with a stiff neck, high fever or a rash can be a sign of meningitis. Get medical help fast.
3. A headache with sudden vision changes, weakness on one side of the body or trouble speaking can be a sign of a stroke. Call emergency services.
4. A headache with confusion or vomiting after a head injury needs a doctor, not just rest.
5. A headache that gets steadily worse over days or weeks with no clear cause is worth bringing to your doctor.

Save this post, and if any of these match, talk to a doctor right away.
