Your heart wants 5 minutes from you today 💚

The habit: a brisk 5-minute walk every day — after lunch, after a call, or whenever you've been sitting too long.

Brisk = you can still talk, but not sing. Regular activity like this can lower your risk of heart disease and stroke. Adults should aim for about 150 minutes a week, and small chunks count toward it.

Start with 5. When it feels easy, make it 10.

Save this for your next long sitting session, and send it to the friend you'd walk with. 🚶

Sources:
