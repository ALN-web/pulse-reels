Cold or flu? Here are three ways to tell them apart.

1. Speed: flu comes on quickly, often within a few hours. A cold creeps in gradually.
2. Where you feel it: a cold mainly affects your nose and throat. Flu affects more than that, with body aches, a high temperature and exhaustion.
3. How you cope: with a cold, you can usually carry on as normal. Flu can leave you too exhausted to carry on.

Either way, rest, drink plenty of water, and stay home if you have a high temperature.

If flu symptoms do not improve after 7 days, or you feel short of breath, talk to a doctor. Save this for the next time you feel rough.
