120/80… but what do those two numbers actually mean? 🩺

Top number (systolic): the pressure when your heart pumps.
Bottom number (diastolic): the pressure when it rests between beats.

Under 120/80 is considered normal. 140/90 or higher, checked by a professional, is usually considered high. In between, guidelines differ by country (the NHS and CDC use different cut-offs), so ask your doctor what your target is.

High blood pressure usually has no symptoms, so the only way to know your numbers is to measure them.

Get yours checked at a pharmacy or your doctor's office, and save this so you can read your next result. 📌
