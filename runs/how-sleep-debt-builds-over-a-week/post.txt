Sleeping 6 hours when you need 8? Here's how that adds up over a week. 😴

Sleep debt is the gap between the sleep you need and the sleep you get.

CDC example: need 8 hours but get 6, and that's 2 hours of sleep debt that day. Do that for 5 days and the debt grows to 10 hours.

As it builds, your brain and body work less well, and you may feel tired and sleepy during the day. Paying it all back later is risky: even when sleepiness fades, you may not be at your best.

Good news: you don't need to repay it hour for hour. If you were short last night, allow extra time and go to bed a little earlier.

Save this, and talk to a doctor if you feel tired all the time. 📌
