How much water do you really need each day? Here's what the NHS and CDC say.

1. The NHS suggests 6 to 8 cups or glasses of fluid a day.
2. It's not just water: lower-fat milk, lower-sugar drinks, tea and coffee all count too.
3. The CDC says daily needs vary with age, sex and activity, so treat it as a guide.
4. You may need more in hot weather, during exercise, or with a fever, vomiting or diarrhea.
5. The easy check: aim for pale yellow pee. Dark yellow pee can be a sign you need to drink more.

One simple habit: carry a reusable water bottle with you.

Save this, and if dizziness or unusual tiredness won't go away, talk to a doctor.
