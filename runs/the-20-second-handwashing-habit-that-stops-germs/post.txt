Five steps and one 20-second scrub: the handwashing habit that helps stop germs spreading.

1. Wet your hands with clean, running water (warm or cold) and apply soap.
2. Lather the backs of your hands, between your fingers and under your nails.
3. Scrub for at least 20 seconds. No timer? Hum Happy Birthday twice.
4. Rinse well under clean, running water.
5. Dry with a clean towel or an air dryer.

Key times: before you eat, after the toilet, and after you cough, sneeze or blow your nose.

Save this, and share it with someone you live with.
