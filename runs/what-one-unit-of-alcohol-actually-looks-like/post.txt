Is one glass of wine one unit of alcohol? Usually not. 🍷

In the UK, one unit is 10 ml (8 g) of pure alcohol. That's a single 25 ml shot of spirits at 40% ABV.

A standard 175 ml glass of 12% wine is 2.1 units, and a pint of strong lager is 3 units.

The NHS advises no more than 14 units a week, spread over 3 or more days. In the US, a standard drink is bigger: 14 g of pure alcohol. Guidelines differ, so ask your doctor what's right for you.

One easy step: check the strength on the label before you pour.

Save this 📌 and talk to your doctor if you're worried about how much you drink.
